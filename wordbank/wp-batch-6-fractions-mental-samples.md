**ff-5a** · level 5 · compare-fractions-of-different-amounts · needs F4 · Plain · 3 marks · twists: decide

  *Ella: 45 ÷ 5 = 9, 9 × 2 = 18 → Leah: 55 ÷ 5 = 11, 11 × 4 = 44 → £44 − £18 = £26* · **Answer: Leah, £26**
- Tia saves {1/5} of £30. Chloe saves {5/8} of £64. Who saves more money? How much more does that person save?  
  *Tia: 30 ÷ 5 = 6 → Chloe: 64 ÷ 8 = 8, 8 × 5 = 40 → £40 − £6 = £34* · **Answer: Chloe, £34**
- Dev saves {7/10} of £90. Leah saves {3/10} of £60. Who saves more money? How much more does that person save?  
  *Dev: 90 ÷ 10 = 9, 9 × 7 = 63 → Leah: 60 ÷ 10 = 6, 6 × 3 = 18 → £63 − £18 = £45* · **Answer: Dev, £45**
