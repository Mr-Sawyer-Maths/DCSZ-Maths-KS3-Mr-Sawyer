**ff-1a** · level 1 · frac-add-parts-of-whole · needs F11 · Plain · 2 marks

- Sam eats {1/2} of a cake. Jack eats {1/10} of the same cake. Work out the fraction of the cake that is eaten altogether.  
  *1/2 = 5/10 → 5/10 + 1/10 = 6/10 → 6/10 = 3/5* · **Answer: 3/5**
- Jack eats {1/4} of a cake. Aisha eats {7/10} of the same cake. Work out the fraction of the cake that is eaten altogether.  
  *1/4 = 5/20 and 7/10 = 14/20 → 5/20 + 14/20 = 19/20* · **Answer: 19/20**
- Dev eats {1/3} of a cake. Grace eats {1/2} of the same cake. Work out the fraction of the cake that is eaten altogether.  
  *1/3 = 2/6 and 1/2 = 3/6 → 2/6 + 3/6 = 5/6* · **Answer: 5/6**

**ff-5a** · level 5 · compare-fractions-of-different-amounts · needs F4 · Plain · 3 marks · twists: decide

- Kai saves {1/4} of $40. Dev saves {3/4} of $20. Who saves more money? How much more does that person save?  
  *Kai: 40 ÷ 4 = 10 → Dev: 20 ÷ 4 = 5, 5 × 3 = 15 → $15 − $10 = $5* · **Answer: Dev, $5**
- Isla saves {7/8} of $32. Olivia saves {7/8} of $56. Who saves more money? How much more does that person save?  
  *Isla: 32 ÷ 8 = 4, 4 × 7 = 28 → Olivia: 56 ÷ 8 = 7, 7 × 7 = 49 → $49 − $28 = $21* · **Answer: Olivia, $21**
- Ravi saves {2/5} of $30. Priya saves {1/10} of $50. Who saves more money? How much more does that person save?  
  *Ravi: 30 ÷ 5 = 6, 6 × 2 = 12 → Priya: 50 ÷ 10 = 5 → $12 − $5 = $7* · **Answer: Ravi, $7**
