**nn-4a** · level 4 · neg-two-results-then-difference · needs N13 · Cambridge · 3 marks

- The temperature in Beijing at 6 am was −9°C. By noon it had risen by 5 degrees. At midnight it was 4 degrees lower than at 6 am. Work out the difference between the temperature at noon and the temperature at midnight.

- The temperature in Edinburgh at 6 am was −9°C. By noon it had risen by 14 degrees. At midnight it was 5 degrees lower than at 6 am. Work out the difference between the temperature at noon and the temperature at midnight.

- The temperature in Reykjavik at 6 am was −3°C. By noon it had risen by 4 degrees. At midnight it was 4 degrees lower than at 6 am. Work out the difference between the temperature at noon and the temperature at midnight.  
  *Noon: −3 + 4 = 1 → Midnight: −3 − 4 = −7 → Difference: 1 − (−7) = 1 + 7 = 8* · **Answer: 8°C**
