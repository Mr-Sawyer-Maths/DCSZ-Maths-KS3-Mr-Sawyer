**ra-5b** · level 5 · combine-two-ratios-in-context · needs R11 · Cambridge · 3 marks · hub ext

- In a pond, the ratio of frogs to newts is 1 : 3. The ratio of newts to fish is 5 : 3. There are 30 newts. Work out the number of fish.  
  *frogs : newts = 1 : 3 = 5 : 15 → newts : fish = 5 : 3 = 15 : 9 → frogs : newts : fish = 5 : 15 : 9 → 30 ÷ 15 = 2 → 9 × 2 = 18* · **Answer: 18**
- In a garden, the ratio of roses to tulips is 1 : 3. The ratio of tulips to daisies is 7 : 3. There are 42 tulips. Work out the number of roses.  
  *roses : tulips = 1 : 3 = 7 : 21 → tulips : daisies = 7 : 3 = 21 : 9 → roses : tulips : daisies = 7 : 21 : 9 → 42 ÷ 21 = 2 → 7 × 2 = 14* · **Answer: 14**
- In a wood, the ratio of oak trees to ash trees is 5 : 8. The ratio of ash trees to beech trees is 6 : 7. There are 72 ash trees. Work out the number of beech trees.  
  *oak trees : ash trees = 5 : 8 = 15 : 24 → ash trees : beech trees = 6 : 7 = 24 : 28 → oak trees : ash trees : beech trees = 15 : 24 : 28 → 72 ÷ 24 = 3 → 28 × 3 = 84* · **Answer: 84**
